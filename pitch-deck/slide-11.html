Why these numbers hold
AI cost is capped by design

Usage is sold as a credit allowance and metered per run with prompt caching. Nobody can consume unbounded inference on a fixed fee.

Infrastructure is commodity, not cloud-premium

Backends run as containers on dedicated hosts, not per-service managed plans — which is what makes a £25 tier profitable at all.

The free-to-paid trigger is concrete

Free gets a real live site; their own domain is what costs £25. They upgrade the moment they decide the business is real.

Churn is structurally low

Domain, catalogue, bookings, content and mailing list all live here. Leaving is not a cancellation, it is a migration.

CAC, LTV and payback are targets underpinning the plan, not measured results — the platform has been in free open beta and has spent nothing on acquisition to date. Validating them against real cohorts is the first milestone this round funds.
