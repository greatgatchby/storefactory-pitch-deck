Why Now: The Shift to AI-Native Commerce

Trend 01

Composable commerce is replacing monolithic platforms

Shopify era — rigid, all-in-one platforms
Next era — composable stacks, API-first, owned infrastructure

Modern brands need custom checkout, integrations, and ownership of their stack. Headless adoption is accelerating but implementation remains complex and expensive.

Trend 02

AI can now automate infrastructure and storefronts

Old model — developers manually build storefronts
New model — AI generates and modifies commerce systems

LLMs can now generate production-ready UI and backend configurations, automating tasks that previously required full engineering teams.

Trend 03

The developer ecosystem is now ready to automate

Before — fragmented tools, high integration cost
Now — headless frameworks, APIs, serverless infra

API-first commerce frameworks, modern cloud infrastructure, and serverless deployments make it possible to provision and operate full commerce stacks automatically.

StoreFactory combines these three trends to automate the entire headless ecommerce stack — the startup window that didn't exist 5 years ago.
