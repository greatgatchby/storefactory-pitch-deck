Why this is hard to copy
1
The pipeline is the product

Anyone can prompt a model into a landing page. Turning that into a provisioned, isolated, billable stack — and keeping it running — is a year of edge cases, not a weekend.

2
The AI margin is engineered

Model spend is metered per run with prompt caching, and priced into a credit allowance. Gross margin on AI usage is a design decision here, not something we find out at the end of the month.

3
We own the runtime, so we keep the relationship

Because the business runs on our infrastructure, we can keep changing it forever. Competitors who hand back a repo hand back the customer with it.
